## Your content

You own what you post — activities, photos, comments. By posting, you let us store and show it
back to you and to whoever your visibility settings allow, and nothing more.

## Acceptable use

Don't post content that is illegal, harassing, or infringes someone else's rights. Report
content that violates this via the in-app Report button. We review reports and may remove
content or suspend accounts (`reports` table, reviewed by the team — see
`docs/security-review.md`).

## Blocking

Blocking someone removes any follow relationship between you and hides your activities from
them regardless of your visibility setting.
